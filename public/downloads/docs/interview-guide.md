# Interview guide & notes template

60-minute semi-structured interview. Adapt the language per role. Ask for stories ("tell me about the last time…"), not opinions about the future.

## Before the session
- [ ] Consent form signed (recording yes/no)
- [ ] For participants under 18: guardian consent and guardian present
- [ ] Prototype open at `/demo/` with demo data reset
- [ ] Note-taker ready (use the template below)

## Script

### Intro (5 min)
"Thanks for your time. We're learning how people organise sports schedules. There are no right or wrong answers, and we're not testing you. May I record so I can take fewer notes? You can stop at any time."
- What's your role in sports? Which sport and age groups?
- How many teams / children / venues do you deal with?

### Current process (15 min)
- Walk me through the last time you created or changed a schedule.
- What tools did you use? Could you show me?
- What was the hardest part? Why?
- Roughly how long did it take? What took the longest?
- How did you check for mistakes or clashes?

### Changes & communication (10 min)
- Tell me about the last time a game or practice moved. What triggered it?
- How did you find out / how did you tell others?
- Did anyone miss it or show up at the wrong place? What happened next?
- Which channel do people actually read: app, email, SMS, group chat?

### Calendars & RSVPs (10 min)
- Where do you look first to know where you need to be?
- How do you answer when a coach asks if you're coming?
- Which notifications do you ignore? Why?
- (Parents with several children) How do you handle overlapping schedules?

### Automation & trust (10 min)
- If software proposed a full schedule for you, what would you check before publishing it?
- What would make you trust it? Distrust it?
- **Concept probe:** show the AI assistant proposal. "Think aloud. What do you notice? What would you change? Would you approve this?"

### Wrap-up (5 min)
- If you could fix one thing about sports scheduling, what would it be?
- Anything we should have asked?
- May we contact you for a 30-minute usability session?
