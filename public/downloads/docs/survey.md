# Customer survey questionnaire

**Estimated time:** 5 minutes · **Tool:** any free form tool (e.g. Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, Tally) · **Status:** not yet distributed.

Intro text: "We're researching how sports schedules are organised and shared. Answers are anonymous and take about 5 minutes. Thank you!"

| # | Question | Type | Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is your main role in youth/community sports? | Single choice | League admin · Club/org manager · Coach · Parent/guardian · Athlete (16+) · Facility staff · Other |
| 2 | How many teams or children do you manage schedules for? | Single choice | 1 · 2 · 3–4 · 5+ |
| 3 | Where do you check schedules most often? | Multiple choice | Team app · Phone calendar · Email · Group chat · Website · Printed/PDF · Other |
| 4 | In the last month, how many times did you (or your child) miss an event or arrive at the wrong time or place? | Number | 0–10+ |
| 5 | How do you usually learn about schedule changes? | Multiple choice | Push notification · SMS · Email · Group chat · Word of mouth · Other |
| 6 | How easy is it to RSVP for events today? | Likert 1–5 | Very difficult → Very easy |
| 7 | (Admins/managers) How long does it take to build a tournament schedule? | Single choice | <1h · 1–3h · 3–8h · >1 day · I don't build them |
| 8 | (Admins/managers) How comfortable would you be publishing a computer-generated schedule after reviewing it? | Likert 1–5 | Not at all → Completely |
| 9 | Which would most improve your experience? (pick up to 3) | Rank / multi | Fewer conflicts · Faster changes · Calendar sync · Easier RSVP · Fewer notifications · Better venue info |
| 10 | Anything else about scheduling you'd like to tell us? | Open text | |
| 11 | May we contact you for a follow-up interview? | Yes/No + email | Optional |

## Analysis plan
- Segment Q3–Q6 by role (Q1) and number of teams/children (Q2).
- Q4 tests H3 (multi-child families miss more events).
- Q5 + Q6 test H4 (RSVP friction by channel).
- Q8 informs H5 (trust in automation).
- Code Q10 into themes; count frequency.
