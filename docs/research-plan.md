# SportSync UX research plan

**Status:** Planned, not started. Owner: Maria Zha-Zha C. Bio. Update this file as research happens.

## 1. Background
SportSync is a concept for AI-assisted sports scheduling. Design so far rests on desk research and assumptions (see hypotheses H1–H7 on the Research page). This plan replaces assumptions with evidence before further investment.

## 2. Problem statement
Volunteer coaches, busy parents and league administrators spend too much time building, changing and communicating sports schedules across spreadsheets, group chats and calendar apps. Changes cascade into confusion and missed events.

## 3. Objectives
1. Understand how admins build season and tournament schedules today, and where time is lost.
2. Identify which changes cause the most downstream confusion.
3. Learn how families receive, trust and act on schedule information.
4. Evaluate trust in automatically generated schedules and the explanation needed to approve them.
5. Understand facility constraints and how they're communicated.

## 4. Methods & sample
| Method | Who | n | Duration | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Semi-structured interviews | 3 admins, 3 coaches, 3 parents, 1–2 athletes (with guardian consent), 1–2 facility staff | 10–12 | 45–60 min | Notes, affinity map, updated personas |
| Survey | Members of 2–4 local leagues/clubs | 50+ responses | 5 min | Channel preferences, frequency of missed events |
| Contextual review | Admins share a past schedule (spreadsheet) | 3 | 30 min | Constraint inventory |
| Concept test | Interviewees react to the prototype | 6+ | 15 min (in interview) | Trust & comprehension notes |

## 5. Recruitment
- Channels: local league admins, school sports coordinators, parent groups (with organizer permission).
- Screener: role, sport, number of teams/children, tools used today.
- Incentive: to be decided (budget not approved).
- Ethics: informed consent; minors only with guardian consent and guardian present; record only with permission; store notes without full names.

## 7. Deliverables
Research report · updated personas (replacing provisional ones) · validated/invalidated hypotheses table · updated journey map · revised RICE scores.

## 8. Success criteria for this research
- Each hypothesis marked validated, invalidated or unclear with evidence.
- At least 3 participants per primary role.
- A ranked list of top 5 pain points with frequency.

## 9. Risks
Volunteer time is scarce → offer 30-minute versions. Self-reported data bias → combine with artifact review. Small sample → treat as directional.
