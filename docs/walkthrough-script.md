# Five-minute portfolio walkthrough script

For a screening call or a recorded video. Roughly 650 words spoken at a natural pace.

**[0:00, Home page]**
"Hi, I'm Maria Bio. I design and build digital products, and I'm especially drawn to messy, real-world workflows like scheduling. In the next five minutes I'll show you SportSync, my featured project, and quickly mention the products I've founded."

**[0:20, SportSync case study header]**
"SportSync is an independent concept for leagues, coaches, parents, athletes and facility managers. It's not affiliated with any company. I designed it end to end and built a working prototype. Two honesty notes: the research is planned but not yet run, and the AI is a rule-based simulation."

**[0:45, Problem & strategy sections]**
"The core problem is the ripple effect. One change, like a closed gym, can break five other games and confuse forty families. So my strategy has three parts: prevent conflicts instead of repairing them, explain every automated decision, and meet families in the calendars they already use."

**[1:15, Switch to /demo/]**
"Here's the prototype. No sign-up needed. The dashboard answers an admin's first question: what needs my attention? Open conflicts, pending RSVPs, venue availability."

**[1:35, Create event → conflict]**
"If I create an event on Court A at a time that's already booked, the conflict shows up while I type, with a one-click way to find free times."

**[2:00, Conflicts → Compare alternatives]**
"On the Conflicts page, every issue says what's wrong, who's affected and how to fix it. I can compare three ranked alternatives side by side, with reasons and trade-offs, then confirm with a before-and-after summary that tells me who'll be notified. Everything can be undone."

**[2:40, AI assistant]**
"This is the part I find most interesting. I ask for an eight-team basketball tournament over three weekends, once per day. Before showing results, the assistant shows what it understood. A full round robin doesn't fit in six days, so it switches to pool play plus playoffs and tells me why. I can edit any row, revise in plain language, or approve, and approval is undoable."

**[3:30, Role switch → Parent RSVP]**
"Switching to a parent with two kids on different teams, I can RSVP for each child in one tap, and the coach sees expected attendance immediately."

**[3:50, Calendar sync]**
"Calendar export is real. This downloads a standards-compliant .ics file. Two-way Google or Outlook sync is shown as a simulated OAuth flow, clearly labeled."

**[4:05, Design system / docs]**
"Behind it: a token-based design system with checked contrast and dark mode, nine user flows, 28 screens in three fidelities, a usability test plan, and engineering handoff: PRD, API, schema, QA and a traceability matrix."

**[4:30, Projects page]**
"I also build my own products: WideKonek, a networking, recruitment and freelance marketplace on Next.js and Supabase; ZC Smart Digital, automation and web demos; and Frendora, a privacy-first social concept."

**[4:50, Close]**
"I'd love to bring this mix of systems thinking, research discipline and hands-on building to your scheduling team. Thanks for watching."
